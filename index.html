About Bob

I'm Bob Wierda, founder of Paddle Out AI. I have over 20 years of experience finding patterns in data and communicating them clearly, and that instinct is what led me here.

A few years ago, I started a coffee business with a different goal in mind: giving my son, who has special needs, a meaningful job to grow into when he's older. Building a second business meant finding time I didn't actually have, so I went looking for ways to multiply my effort. That search led me to AI.

I started absorbing everything I could. Before long, other professionals noticed what I was building and started asking me for advice, so what began as me asking questions turned into me explaining answers. Somewhere in that shift, I realized I'd learned enough to genuinely help other businesses navigate their way through AI too. Paddle Out AI is that help, made available to you.
